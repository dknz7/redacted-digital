Page structure — 7 sections, three flips

Down from eleven. The stripe pattern is the point: dark carries the pitch, paper carries the proof. Every flip is a gear change, which is what stops a long page feeling long.

ASPHALT — pitch, voice, momentum
PAPER — proof, detail, redaction
FLIP MARKER
[ 01 ]// OPERATION FOCUS

Hero

One claim, one line of plain English, one button. Big condensed caps, orange on the payoff line. The single cinematic moment on the page.

[ 02 ]// CAPABILITY TICKER

Thin scrolling mono strip. Cheap rhythm, reads as part of the hero, buys a breath before the pitch.

[ 03 ]// THREAT ASSESSMENT

The problem

Three pains, not four. Missed calls, page four of Google, slow replies. Scroll-triggered ████ reveals.

[ 04 ]// CASE FILES · DECLASSIFIED

Proof

Real numbers on real jobs. Merges the old Social Proof and Portfolio into one hit. Redaction works properly here because it's paper.

[ 05 ]// CAPABILITIES

What we actually do

Four or five, plain language. Absorbs the old Solution and Why Us sections, which were saying the same thing twice.

[ 06 ]// THE METHOD

How it works

Briefing → Build → Deploy → Grow. Four steps, horizontal on desktop, stacked on mobile. Kills the "what am I signing up for" objection.

[ 07 ]// TERMS OF ENGAGEMENT

What it costs

New. From $X/mo, three tiers named, link to the full pricing page. The older buyer will not ring you without a number.

[ 08 ]// MISSION BRIEFING

Final CTA + footer

Operative seal, one button, contact details. Merged — a standalone CTA above a footer is two goodbyes.

What got cut

  • SOLUTION — merged into Capabilities. Was restating the pitch.
  • WHY US — merged into Capabilities. Four cards saying "we're good".
  • PROBLEMS WE FIX — it's an FAQ. Belongs on a subpage.
  • RESOURCES — your own doc calls it a placeholder at launch. Don't ship an empty room.
  • SOCIAL PROOF — folded into Case Files. Testimonials sit next to the numbers they belong to.

What got added

  • PRICING TEASER — the one genuine addition. A tradie who can't find a number assumes he can't afford you and closes the tab.

The argument

  • Eleven → seven is a cut, not a trim. Every section removed was repeating another one.
  • Three flips means the page changes gear three times. That's what keeps a scroll from feeling endless.
  • Paper carries every fact you want believed. Dark carries every claim you want felt.
  • Target: roughly half Firecrawl's height.
